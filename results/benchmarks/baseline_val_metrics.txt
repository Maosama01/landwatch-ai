Limitations (read before comparing with a learned model)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Pixel differencing reacts to ANY brightness difference, not only to real change.
  Illumination and sun-angle differences, shadows, seasonal vegetation, haze and
  small registration offsets between A and B all produce false positives.
* LEVIR-CD labels only building changes (construction and demolition). Genuine
  non-building changes (roads, bare soil, vegetation, water, vehicles) are scored
  as false positives under this protocol.
* Only luminance is compared: a new structure whose brightness is close to what was
  there before is missed (false negatives), whatever its colour or texture.
* Otsu always splits the difference histogram into two classes, so even an image
  with little or no real change gets its most-different pixels flagged.
* Metrics come from one global confusion matrix, so images with many pixels of
  change weigh more. In the per-image CSV, precision/recall/F1/IoU are 0.0 when
  their denominator is 0 (BinaryChangeMetrics convention), e.g. F1 = 0.0 for an
  image with no true and no predicted change.
* Latency depends on the machine and JVM; treat it as indicative only.
